    How the agent decides

    The decision

    What price, or offer, should each perfume have this week?

    Decision variables: selling price per perfume, and offer % for slow movers.

    Principle of choice: stay competitive with Golden Scent, Faces and Sephora while never going below the minimum margin (20% mid-range, 30% luxury).

    Type: semistructured. The agent recommends, the owner approves.

    The rules (checked in this order)

    First, a data check: if the data contradicts itself (for example stock is 0 but bottles were sold), do nothing and ask the owner to check.

    1. Protect the margin: if the cost went up and the price is below the minimum, raise to the minimum price.
    2. High demand, low stock: under 14 days of stock and sales steady or rising → raise 5%.
    3. Slow mover: more than 60 days of stock → 10% offer; more than 90 days → 15% offer. Never below the minimum price.
    4. Competitor is cheaper: match the cheapest competitor, but never go below the minimum price.
    5. Otherwise keep the price.

    Minimum price = cost ÷ (1 − minimum margin). Any change over 15% is flagged for owner review.

    Four parts of the system

    • Data: products, weekly sales, costs, competitor prices (data dictionary and CSV files in the data folder).
    • Models: minimum price, days of stock, profit forecast, what-if and goal seek.
    • Knowledge: the pricing rules above (agent.js).
    • Interface: this website and the weekly briefing.
    • User: the owner of Scent Lab, who approves every change.

    When not to trust me

    • Competitor prices are only as fresh as the last price check (see the date next to the button), and bottle sizes may differ between sites. A competitor sale can end tomorrow.
    • Sales, stock and costs are simulated, not from a real shop.
    • The profit forecast uses assumptions (price sensitivity 1.2, competitor sensitivity 3). Real customers may react differently.
    • I do not know about brand rules, supplier deals, or events I was not told about.
    • If a change is flagged “Owner review”, or confidence is Low, check it yourself before approving.
    • I never change a price on my own. A person always decides.
